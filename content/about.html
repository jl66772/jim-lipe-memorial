Your dad was born in Salinas, California, and grew up between the Salinas Valley and Lake Tahoe. Later he made his home in Salinas and Tahoma.

He spent his working life in trucking and agriculture. He started his own company, LipeLine Trucking. In 2007 he joined his father and brother in buying NH3 Service Company, a family business your great-great-grandfather Ralph Iliff started in 1938. Your dad led it as president and CEO until it was sold in 2015, carrying his family's story forward in the Salinas Valley.

Your dad loved adventure. He skied, heli-skied in Alaska, rode snowmobiles through Desolation Wilderness, took on 24-hour mountain bike rides, and rode dirt bikes through Baja. The people closest to him described a man who lived "at full throttle."

When your sister Katherine was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes, he became a dedicated advocate and fundraiser for Breakthrough T1D (formerly JDRF), helping in the search for a cure.

Your dad is survived by you and your siblings, Katherine and Stephen, his sister Julie, his brothers William and Michael, and extended family. His parents, Jim Sr. and Penny, passed away before him.
